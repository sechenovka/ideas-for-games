# Development Status

## Unbound

Active development.

Scientific space simulation focused on realistic systems, physics and experimental gameplay.

---

## Arcanix

Near completion.

The main gameplay systems are being finished.

Remaining tasks:
- story integration;
- 2D assets;
- animations;
- final content polish.

---

## NightShift

Personal experimental horror project.

Focused on atmosphere, psychological tension and exploration.

---

## Remote Horizon

Concept stage.

A sci-fi project about remote humanoid robots, planetary exploration and building human infrastructure beyond Earth.

---

## Void Salvage

Prototype in development.

Offline space fleet campaign: assemble ships from salvaged parts, run missions, raid stations and planetary bases. Manual armada control by default with a full-AI autopilot toggle. Optional online weekend clan wars.

Playable physics prototype: `void-salvage/prototype/` — open `index.html` in a browser.

---

## Scrap Wars

Concept stage.

Post-collapse PvP vehicle-builder: build war machines from scavenged parts, raid convoys and player bases manually, fight in clan sector wars and weekly titan battles. Shares its parts/physics tech with Void Salvage.
